# PromoFinder rubric

Score every criterion from 0 to 4. Use the full range; a dramatic line alone is not
enough to make a usable promo. Scores 1 and 3 sit between the anchors.

| Criterion | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook (first 3s) | Setup, camera movement | A clear but ordinary opening beat (an accusation starting, a question asked) | Shocking line, reveal, violence or threat that stops a stranger |
| Cliffhanger | Resolved, payoff or mood | Tension is open but the stakes are vague | Ends mid-threat or pending decision with explicit unanswered stakes |
| Standalone | Needs prior episodes | The conflict is followable but who someone is stays unclear | A cold viewer can say who wants what and who opposes them |
| Escalation | Flat | Some rise in tension | Stakes rise throughout the clip |
| Density | Dead air | Mostly useful | Every unit earns its place |

A post-worthy candidate must score at least 2 on Hook, Cliffhanger, and Standalone.
Among gated candidates, later ranking uses Hook 0.3, Cliffhanger 0.3, Standalone
0.2, Escalation 0.2; Density is a tie-breaker. These weights are unvalidated.
